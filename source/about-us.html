Meaning of our logo

The trident has long been a powerful symbol of strength, resilience, and commitment...

In battle, warriors wielded it with a dedication to putting the mission first and protecting others above their own safety. Now, as Mandatum Fellows reach the end of their military careers, they prepare to lay down the trident for the first time and embark on a new chapter.

For the first time, these warriors are asked to shift their focus from the mission to themselves, trading selflessness in battle for the courage to invest in their well-being and future. Putting down the trident represents a momentous shift—a chance to prioritize their growth and a renewed sense of self after years of dedicated service to our nation.

The Mandatum Foundation honors this transition with a downward-facing trident as its logo, symbolizing a commitment to supporting each Fellow’s personal journey. Through mentorship, resources, and industry partnerships, Mandatum ensures that each Fellow’s transition from military to civilian life is purposeful and supported. This commitment allows them to meet new challenges with the same resilience and strength they carried into service. Mandatum is there every step of the way, helping Fellows build a fulfilling life beyond the battlefield.

Bill Ahern

Bill Ahern

Building on a career of service, leadership, and community, Bill joined The Mandatum Foundation as CEO in May 2026. He has a long history of working with nonprofits, having served on the boards of Habitat affiliates in Union County, North Carolina, and Sarasota County, Florida, as well as serving as the CEO of Habitat Greater Raleigh. After growing up as an "Army brat," Bill went to high school in western Maryland and then to college at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC), majoring in Economics. He was also a four-year starter on the baseball team, earning All-America honors and being inducted into UMBC’s Athletic Hall of Fame.

Most of Bill’s professional career was spent with the Federal Reserve System in a variety of management positions in technology and operations, serving at the Baltimore office for 20 years and another 7 at the Charlotte office. After retiring from the Fed, Bill owned and managed a retail business in Venice, Florida, for 7 years while also being deeply involved in the community with the Venice Area Chamber of Commerce and the City of Venice’s Economic Development Advisory Council, before becoming CEO of Habitat Raleigh in 2019.

Bill retired in 2023 and moved back to Florida with his wife, Beth—but it wasn't long before he realized fully retiring was premature. After a chance encounter with a member of Mandatum’s influential leadership team, Bill began working with Mandatum’s Board and was soon persuaded to come out of retirement to take on the CEO role. Bill and Beth have three grown children who live in Charlotte, North Carolina, and Sarasota, Florida. For leisure, Bill enjoys getting out on his kayak, catching blue crabs, and steaming them Maryland-style.

Damon George

Damon George serves as the Chairman of the Board, driving initiatives that advance the organization's mission and foster impactful partnerships to improve transition outcomes for elite military personnel. Drawing on his experience leading high-performing teams and working in defense technology, Damon offers strategic insight, operational acumen, and a broad network within the defense sector to support the foundation’s mission of helping retiring Naval Special Operations members successfully transition to fulfilling civilian careers.
Damon currently serves as a Business Development Manager for Vatn Systems. Vatn designs, develops, and manufactures affordable, modular, and attritable autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) that operate in cooperative swarms to provide the mass, stealth, and range needed to address maritime challenges across the conflict spectrum.
Before joining The Mandatum Foundation, Damon dedicated 21 years to serving as a SEAL in Naval Special Warfare. Over the course of his extensive military career, he led teams through high-pressure missions, where failure was not an option. Damon not only excelled in these demanding environments, but he also made it a priority to mentor his subordinates, encouraging them to embrace opportunities for personal and professional development.
Damon holds a Master’s degree in Organizational Leadership from Johns Hopkins University, where he deepened his understanding of leadership principles and organizational dynamics. As Co-Founder of The Mandatun Foundation, Damon helps veterans navigate the transition to civilian life, guiding them to explore fulfilling career paths that not only offer personal satisfaction but also contribute to the growth and success of the industries they join. His commitment to supporting service members extends beyond simply finding jobs; it’s about helping them build meaningful careers that benefit both their families and the broader community.
Outside of work, Damon enjoys playing sports with his two boys, sitting by the fire, or starting a new home renovation project.
