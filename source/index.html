Providing the tools for success

At The Mandatum Foundation, we are dedicated to ensuring retiring Special Operations members transition smoothly into civilian life. Through hands-on work experience, mentorship, and exposure to new skills or trades, we help them bridge resume gaps, explore certification pathways, and, in many cases, secure meaningful post-service employment.

Our mission

Empowering Special Operations Members for Success Beyond Service

We prepare retiring elite Special Operations members for fulfilling civilian careers, empowering them to shape their next chapter with confidence and purpose.

Our vision

To be the trusted partner for retiring elite Special Operations members empowering purposeful transitions and building a strong and supportive network beyond service.

Our values

Fellows First

We prioritize the well-being, success, and growth of our Fellows above all else. Every decision we make is guided by our commitment to their transition, ensuring they receive the support, resources, and opportunities needed to thrive in their next mission—life beyond service.

Trust

Trust is the foundation of our relationships—with our Fellows, donors, corporate partners, and team. We operate with integrity, transparency, and accountability, ensuring that every action we take upholds the confidence placed in us.

Teamwork

Success is never achieved alone. We believe in collaboration, unity, and shared purpose. By fostering a strong network of Fellows, mentors, supporters, and partners, we create lasting impact and ensure every transition is met with unwavering support.

Relentless Improvement

We embrace a mindset of continuous growth and innovation. Just as our Fellows have dedicated themselves to excellence, we strive to refine our programs, expand our reach, and challenge ourselves to provide the best possible resources for their journey forward.

Pillar of support

Industry Immersion

Industry Immersions are intensive, unpaid experiences lasting one to one-and-a-half weeks. Fellows explore civilian organizations aligned with their career goals while delivering real value. By shadowing executives and working with cross-functional teams, Fellows gain firsthand insight into leadership, culture, and operations—while host organizations experience the proven leadership and results-driven mindset Fellows bring from elite Special Operations service.

02
Pillar of support

Executive Coaching

Executive Coaching delivers personalized, one-on-one guidance tailored to each Fellow’s transition and career objectives. Coaching builds confidence, sharpens focus, and accelerates entry into civilian roles. Support includes assessments, professional branding, interview preparation, networking strategy, and warm introductions—driving clarity and momentum toward meaningful career outcomes.

03
Pillar of support

Strategic Networking

Strategic Networking equips Fellows to build intentional, high-value relationships in the civilian workforce. Through a structured, values-driven approach, Fellows expand their professional networks beyond military service. Curated roundtables, workshops, and small-group engagements with executive leaders provide insight into civilian career paths, workplace norms, and leadership expectations—building social capital, confidence, and context for successful transition.

04
Pillar of support

Family Support

Family Support recognizes that successful transition depends on strong support at home. The program prioritizes family well-being through mentorship, practical resources, and financial assistance that ease the challenges of transition and relocation. Families are supported as they navigate career change, new communities, and life after service, with tools that promote stability, resilience, and connection. By strengthening the family unit, we empower Fellows and their families to move forward together with confidence and purpose.

The Mandatum Foundation

The Mandatum Foundation was established in 2022 by military service members who have walked the same path as the exceptional individuals we serve.

Designed exclusively for a select cadre of elite professionals, we provide a concierge-level transition experience, ensuring a seamless and strategic shift from active duty to high-impact civilian careers.

With deep expertise in guiding the best of the best, The Mandatum Foundation delivers the connections, insights, and support necessary for our Fellows — and their families — to thrive beyond the uniform.

Fellowship FAQs

What advantages does a company gain by hosting a Fellowship?

Hosting a fellowship provides a company with access to highly skilled and talented Fellows at no cost. Throughout the Fellowship, company leadership can evaluate their capabilities in a real-world setting while benefiting from their expertise. Although there is no obligation to extend a job offer, many of our Fellows secure full-time positions upon completing their Fellowships, making this a low-risk opportunity to discover top talent.

What is the process for hosting a Fellowship at a company?

Our Mandatum Transition Team (MTT) works closely with company representatives to understand the Fellowship role, ideal candidate profile, and company culture. We then facilitate a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between the company and our MTT. Once the MOU is signed, we provide resumes of qualified candidates who align with the company’s needs. The organization has the opportunity to interview and select the best fit before extending a Fellowship offer.

What is the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)?

The MOU is a formal agreement that defines the key parameters of the Fellowship, including its purpose, expectations, and responsibilities of both the sponsoring organization and the Fellow. It ensures clarity and alignment between all parties. Once finalized, the document is signed by the appropriate company representatives and our team.

Why is the Fellowship unpaid? Can the company compensate the Fellow?

The Fellowship is unpaid because the service member remains on active duty and continues to receive full military pay throughout the immersion period. Per military regulations, service members cannot accept compensation from an external organization while participating in the program.

How should a company engage and utilize a Fellow during the immersion?

The immersion experience should be tailored to each Fellow based on their skills, interests, and career goals. We encourage companies to provide exposure to multiple departments, roles, and responsibilities to give the Fellow a well-rounded understanding of the organization. While job shadowing can be valuable, hands-on experience is preferred whenever possible. We recommend pairing the Fellow with a mentor—ideally a veteran within the organization—to provide guidance and support throughout the immersion.

Who has authority over the Fellow?

As an active-duty service member, the Fellow remains under the authority of his military chain of command. However, with his command’s approval to participate in the Fellowship, he also reports to the supervisor designated by the hosting company or organization for day-to-day responsibilities and guidance.

How long does the Fellowship last?

Fellowships typically span seven to ten days.

Will the Fellow require special accommodations for a disability?

Most Fellows require little to no special accommodations. However, if a Fellow has a condition that may impact his work or requires specific accommodations, he will discuss his needs with the company in advance to ensure a supportive and successful Fellowship experience.

What if the Fellowship isn’t working out? Can the company terminate it?

We take great care in matching each Fellow with the right opportunity and encourage hosting organizations to invest time in the selection process. However, in the rare case that the Fellowship is not a good fit, the company has the option to terminate it. Likewise, if the experience does not meet the Fellow’s expectations or needs, they may also choose to end the Fellowship.

If challenges arise, we ask that the company reach out to our team to discuss concerns and explore possible solutions before making a final decision.

TESTIMOnials

Chris Eichas, Retired EOD

Shortly after committing to transitioning out of the military, I realized that I truly did not know what I wanted to pursue next, or how to narrow down my options. More importantly, I did not know how to clearly articulate and package up the past 20 years of military service and make myself a solid candidate for a potential company. In need of help, I was connected with the Mandatum Foundation and this quickly changed. I received executive coaching and had calls with other transitioning veterans who were in different phases of the transition process. These interactions gave me confidence and taught me the value of patience. The folks at the Mandatum Foundation helped me build my LinkedIn profile and resume. I received one-on-one coaching, which was the most beneficial to me during my transition. This coaching helped me determine what was important to me and my family, how to connect with people, and the values I wanted from a future employer and company.

Throughout this process, I learned that it is ok to say no. I had some excellent opportunities presented to me, but I knew they were not exactly what I needed to fit my priorities.

I have found a position with a great company that demonstrates the values I share and has built a great team of folks I am excited to work with. I can maximize my military experience and utilize my education with a company that conducts business in a specialized field I truly enjoy. Moving forward I intend to stay engaged with The Mandatum Foundation. I am willing to help other transitioning veterans in any way possible. I am happy to share my experience through this process and hopefully take some of the stress off of others. This can become an extremely stressful process, but at the end of the day, it should be fun.

Clint York, Retired Navy SEAL

Their ability to connect veterans with key industry leaders, mentors, and opportunities is second to none.

Mandatum ensures that operators like me don’t just transition, we thrive. Your support fuels a mission that empowers veterans to bring their leadership, resilience, and experience into industries where they can continue making a difference.

I’m incredibly grateful for the doors Mandatum has opened, and I encourage those looking to support a truly impactful organization to invest in their mission. It’s not just a donation. It’s a direct contribution to the future success of our nation’s most capable leaders.
